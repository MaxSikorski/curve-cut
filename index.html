About Curve Cut

Hi — I'm Max. I run a large online 3D printing meetup where we print a lot, and especially BIG things.

Curve Cut combines ideas from a couple of projects I love — the Polycarver and LuBan3D — but those are Windows and desktop apps. I wanted something that works right in the browser: nothing to download, and it even runs completely offline if you want.

So I built Curve Cut: load any 3D model (STL, OBJ, or 3MF), cut it along curves instead of hard flat planes, and — with Big Print — scale it up, auto-slice it for your printer, and print BIG.
